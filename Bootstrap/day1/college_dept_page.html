About the Department

Established in 1995, the Department of Computer Science is committed to excellence in teaching, research, and student development. We offer comprehensive undergraduate and graduate programs designed to bridge theoretical foundations with cutting-edge industry applications.

Our curriculum covers Artificial Intelligence, Data Science, Cyber Security, and Software Engineering, ensuring our graduates are ready to tackle tomorrow's challenges.

  • Industry-aligned curriculum
  • State-of-the-art research labs
  • 95% placement record
