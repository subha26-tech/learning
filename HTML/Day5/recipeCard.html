Masala Dosa

Ingredients

  • 2 cups dosa batter
  • 2 boiled potatoes
  • 1 onion (sliced)
  • 1 green chili
  • 1 tsp mustard seeds
  • 1 tsp turmeric powder
  • Salt to taste
  • Oil for cooking

Steps

  1. Heat oil in a pan and add mustard seeds.
  2. Saute onions, green chili, and turmeric powder.
  3. Add boiled potatoes, mash, and mix well.
  4. Spread dosa batter thinly on a hot pan.
  5. Place potato filling inside and fold the dosa.
  6. Serve hot with chutney and sambar.

Enjoy your crispy Masala Dosa with traditional sides!
