For decades, we blamed aliens and Atlantis. Science says the Bermuda Triangle story is very different

The Bermuda Triangle has long been a magnet for mystery. Tales of vessels vanishing without distress calls and planes disappearing mid-flight have fuelled theories about alien abductions, time warps, and lost civilisations. The area, marked by the points of Florida, Bermuda and Puerto Rico, has been cast as a danger zone. Australian scientist Karl Kruszelnicki disagrees. He has spent years dismantling these claims, arguing the disappearances can be explained by environmental hazards, human error, and simple probability. His view is backed by the US National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration (NOAA), Lloyd's of London, and the US Navy.
