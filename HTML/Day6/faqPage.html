Installation

How do I install the software?

Download the installer from our website, run the setup file, and follow the on‑screen instructions.

Do I need admin rights?

Yes, administrator privileges are required to complete installation.

Updates

How often are updates released?

We release updates every quarter, with occasional hotfixes for urgent issues.

Do I need to update manually?

Manual updates were required before 2024. Now, updates are automatic and run in the background.

Support

How can I contact support?

You can reach us via live chat, email, or the support ticket system in your account dashboard.

Is 24/7 support available?

Yes, our support team is available around the clock for all premium users.
