Purpose of Each Tag

Tag Purpose
<header> Contains introductory content such as the website title, logo, or a short introduction. It appears at the top of the page or a section.
<nav> Holds navigation links that help users move to different sections or pages of the website.
<main> Contains the primary content of the webpage. There should typically be only one `
` element per page.
<section> Groups related content together. Each section usually has its own heading, such as "About Me" or "Projects".
<footer> Contains information displayed at the bottom of the page, such as copyright, contact information, or social media links.
