Task 11

Italian Red Sauce Pasta

Ingredients

  • Pasta: 200g (about 2 cups) of any pasta shape (like penne or spaghetti).
  • Olive Oil: 2 tablespoons.
  • Garlic: 4 cloves, finely chopped.
  • Onion: 1 medium, finely chopped.
  • Tomatoes: 4 large, finely chopped (or 2 cups canned tomato puree).
  • Seasoning: 1 tsp salt, ½ tsp black pepper, 1 tsp sugar (to balance acidity).
  • Herbs: 1 tsp chili flakes, 2 tsp mixed Italian herbs (oregano, basil).
  • Garnish: Fresh chopped parsley and grated parmesan cheese.

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Boil the Pasta: Bring a large pot of heavily salted water to a rolling boil. Add your pasta and cook until al dente (tender but still firm to the bite). This usually takes 8 - 10 minutes. Save one fourth cup of the starchy pasta water before draining!
  2. Saute the Aromatics: Heat olive oil in a large pan over medium heat. Add the chopped garlic and onions, cooking for 2 - 3 minutes until the onions are soft and fragrant.
  3. Make the Sauce: Add the chopped tomatoes to the pan. Cook for 5 - 7 minutes until the tomatoes break down into a thick sauce.
  4. Season: Stir in the salt, black pepper, sugar, chili flakes, and Italian seasoning. Let it simmer for 2 minutes to let the flavors blend.
  5. Combine: Transfer the boiled pasta to the pan. Toss it vigorously so the sauce coats every piece. If the sauce is too thick, pour in a splash of the saved pasta water to loosen it.
  6. Serve: Turn off the heat, mix in a handful of grated parmesan cheese, and top with fresh parsley. Serve immediately!

HTML List Type Explaination

Use the Unordered List tag when the sequence of items does not matter. You can pick up the Garlic before the pasta without changing the final outcome.

Use the Ordered List tag when the sequence of items is critical to the outcome. Cooking instructions must follow a step-by-step sequencial order, as you cannot add seasoning before boil the pasta.
