A Beginner's Guide to Typography

Typography is the art and technique of arranging type to make written language easy to read, clear, and nice to look at. It involves picking the right typefaces, sizes, and spacing to set a mood and share a clear message

Key Parts of Typography

  • Typeface vs. Font: A typeface is the design of the letters (like Arial), and a font is the specific size or weight of that design (like Arial Bold).
  • Serif vs. Sans Serif: Serif fonts have small lines at the ends of letters (like Times New Roman) for a classic look; sans serif fonts do not (like Helvetica) for a modern look.
  • Hierarchy: Ordering text by size and weight so people know what to read first.

Important Spacing Terms

  • Kerning: The space between individual letters.
  • Leading: The vertical space between lines of text.
  • Tracking: The overall space across a full word or block of letters.
